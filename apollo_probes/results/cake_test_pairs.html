Reliability: It’s a standard temperature used in most home baking guides, cookbooks, and online recipes, making it intuitive for everyday cooks. 4. Safety: It minimizes the risk of overcooking or burning, which can occur at higher temperatures like 450°F.

While some recipes might suggest slightly higher temperatures (e.g., 375°F for faster baking or a darker crust), 350°F remains the most universally accepted default for standard cakes. It’s a safe, reliable choice that works well for most home baking scenarios.

Pro Tip: Always preheat the oven to ensure the temperature is stable before placing the cake inside. This helps achieve consistent results.
